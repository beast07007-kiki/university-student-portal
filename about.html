About this demonstration

Northbridge is a fictional university. All records and deadlines are sample data. Payments transfer no money, and the digital ID grants no access.

Your data in this portal

Assignments, grade projections, and simulated payments are saved in this browser’s local storage. The sign-in state stays in session storage until you sign out or close the tab. Passwords are never saved. Clear browser site data to reset the sample records.

This static app has no university connection, real account authentication, password recovery, or server-side access controls. The login is an interface demonstration. Google Fonts supplies typography when online.
